Privacy policy

Vault · last updated 25 November 2026

Vault lets you browse the posts you saved on Instagram and the tweets you liked on X, using the data export files those services give you. It is built so that your data stays with you.

Your posts stay on your device

When you choose an export file, Vault reads it inside your browser. The posts, captions, likes, favourites and other marks are stored only in your browser's storage on that device. They are never sent to Vault or to anyone else. Vault has no server and no database of its own.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, Vault asks Google for two things:

  • Its own app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). Vault saves one small file there with your favourites and gone marks, so they stay the same on your other devices. Vault cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive, and the folder is hidden from your normal Drive view.
  • Your email address, only to show which Google account is connected.

This connection goes straight from your browser to Google. Vault does not store your Google sign-in anywhere except your own browser, and the sign-in expires after an hour. You can turn sync off in Vault's Settings at any time, and remove Vault's access completely at myaccount.google.com/connections, which also deletes its hidden folder.

Vault's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is used only to keep your favourites in sync for you. It is not shared, sold, used for ads, or used to train AI models.

Previews

When you show a preview of a post, Vault loads Instagram's or X's own embed of that post, so those services can see that the post was viewed, as they would if you opened it on their sites.

Files you save

Save a copy of everything and backup files are created on your device and saved to your downloads. Where they go after that is up to you.

Removing your data

In Vault's Settings you can remove your posts from the device. Clearing your browser's site data for Vault removes everything it stored. Turning off Google Drive sync and removing Vault's access in your Google account removes the synced file.
